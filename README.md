My Love,

There’s no easy way to write this, and perhaps no set of words will ever be enough. Still, I owe you the honesty I failed to give you when it mattered most.

What I did is unforgivable, I know that. I betrayed your trust. I crossed a line that should have never been approached, let alone crossed. And even now, the weight of my choices sits heavy on my chest, because I know I hurt the one person who breathed life back into me.

Before you, I was entangled in something that drained me completely. I was lost, emotionally withered, and too weak to free myself from it. Then you came along, full of warmth, light, and quiet strength. You reminded me what it meant to feel alive, to feel seen. And in that renewal, I made a selfish choice. I kept you close while hiding the truth, convincing myself that loving you could somehow justify my deception.

But it didn’t. It never will.

I should have walked away from her the moment I realized what you meant to me. I should have faced the discomfort, the conflict, and told you everything. Instead, I acted out of fear; fear of losing you, fear of confrontation, fear of facing myself. And now, I’ve hurt the very person who showed me what real love is.

If there’s even a sliver of space in your heart for forgiveness if you're willing to offer me one final chance, I promise I won’t waste it. I will give you the kind of love that is honest, consistent, and whole. The kind of love you deserve, free of shadows and secrets.

But if your answer is silence, or goodbye, I’ll understand. I’ll carry the loss as a reminder of the pain I caused… and the love I lost through my own doing.

Still, I hope. And I’m here, heart in hand willing to do the work, if you’ll let me.

With all my regret,
Jacob:)
